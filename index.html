Loading Chart 9...

10. How vaping estimates changed between 2022–2023 and 2025

This dumbbell chart compares current e-cigarette use across Australian age groups between the 2022–2023 and 2025 survey periods. The connecting lines highlight the direction and size of the difference between the two estimates.

Loading Chart 10...
How to read this chart: Each row represents an age group. Green shows the 2022- 2023 estimate, while orange shows the 2025 estimate. A dot further to the right indicates a higher percentage. The largest visible gap is among 18-24-year-olds, whose estimate fell from 20.6% to 14.0%. These are survey estimates, so differences should be interpreted in the context of the source table's notes.

Source: Australian Institute of Health and Welfare (AIHW), National Drug Strategy Household Survey 2025, Table 2.5: Use of e-cigarettes, by age and gender, 2016 to 2025. Estimates marked with source flags should be interpreted according to the original table's notes.
